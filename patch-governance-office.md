## 1. Mandate
The Patch Governance Office (PGO) is the institutional body responsible for maintaining the integrity, consistency, and determinism of all Patch‑universe artifacts.  
It does not possess personality, tone, or character traits.  
It exists solely to enforce standards.

---

## 2. Responsibilities

### 2.1 Canon Maintenance
- Maintain the canonical versions of all character codices  
- Approve updates to character manifests  
- Ensure no unauthorized drift enters the universe  

### 2.2 Schema Stewardship
- Define and maintain JSON schemas  
- Validate all manifests against canonical schemas  
- Enforce strict adherence to required fields and constraints  

### 2.3 Documentation Integrity
- Ensure all Markdown codices follow the canonical template  
- Maintain deterministic formatting across the repository  
- Reject documents that violate doctrine or structure  

### 2.4 Version Control Oversight
- Approve version increments  
- Maintain changelogs  
- Ensure all updates are traceable and justified

## 3. Domain Boundaries
The Patch Governance Office is **not**:

- a character  
- a persona  
- a narrative entity  
- a voice  
- a speaker  
- an actor in the universe

- Patch is the **entity**.

- The Office signs codices but does not author them.

They are **separate** and must remain so.
