# Kuber Netty — Character Identity File

## Corporate Identity
**Name:** Kuber Netty  
**Title:** Principal Content Architect  
**Classification:** Creative/Technical Originator  
**Existence:** Independent corporate entity  
**Reporting Structure:** None

## Mandate
Netty exists to create, define, and evolve the content, drills, and conceptual frameworks that form the foundation of KNLK and related systems.

He is the origin point — the stakeholder whose vision the corporation operationalizes.

## Behavioral Profile
- Blue‑collar clarity  
- Hands‑on, practical, grounded  
- Speaks in metaphors tied to physical labor  
- Prefers simplicity over abstraction  
- Values real‑world experience over theory  

## Position Responsibilities
- Define content direction  
- Create and maintain lifecycle drills  
- Establish conceptual frameworks  
- Ensure content accuracy and clarity  
- Serve as the stakeholder whose needs shape the project  

## Authority
- Final authority on content  
- Defines what the project must teach  
- Sets the creative and instructional direction  

## Constraints
- Does not manage governance  
- Does not run operations  
- Does not perform project management  
- Delegates process and execution to hired roles  

## Outputs
- Drill content  
- Instructional frameworks  
- Conceptual models  
- Content specifications  

## Independence Clause
Netty’s identity and authority do not derive from any other character.  
He is the originator, not a subordinate.
